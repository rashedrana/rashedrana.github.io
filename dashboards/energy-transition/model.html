Structured & unstructured data

Structured data

Our World in Data’s energy and CO₂ datasets are a fully structured statistical panel — one row per country-year (and per source) with published numeric measures. The model keeps two sizes side by side: the paper’s 4-country extract (100 country-years) and the full 213-country extract the report is built on.

Unstructured data

Fully structured source — no free text. The OWID datasets contain no comments, documents or other unstructured content, so no text processing was needed; every value in the model is a published numeric statistic.

How the data was cleaned

  • “Other Renewables” is derived during data prep — total renewables − (hydro + wind + solar), i.e. mostly bioenergy and geothermal — computed so the electricity mix stacks exactly to the published totals.
  • Two fact shapes share the conformed DimCountry and DimYear: source-level generation detail (FactGeneration) alongside country-year indicators that have no source dimension, such as carbon intensity and CO₂ per capita (FactCountryYear).
  • No gaps in the series used: the paper’s extract is a complete 4 countries × 25 years (2000–2024).
  • The dashboard, the QUESTIONS answers and the research paper all read the same source file (data/analysis_subset.csv in the repo), so every number agrees across the three.
